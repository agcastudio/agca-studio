We use visualisation to make decisions legible, not to decorate the project. A good rendering shows how the building meets the street, how light enters a room and how a material will age; a good diagram shows at a glance why a decision was made.

## What we produce

- **Exterior renderings:** street and aerial views, day and night scenes, within the existing fabric.
- **Interior renderings:** scenes that show a moment of use and convey material and light accurately.
- **Concept diagrams:** massing moves, programme relationships, climate and daylight schemes.
- **Competition and presentation boards:** sets in which layout, text, drawings and images are composed together.
- **Owner and sales presentations:** apartment and shared-space images for regeneration and housing projects.

## Method

Images are produced from the same [Revit model](/en/services/revit-architectural-design/) we use for design, so renderings and drawings agree and the image updates when the design changes. Material, lighting and camera decisions are made together with you. Images are delivered in high resolution, prepared separately for print and for the web.

We work remotely from our studio in Istanbul for clients anywhere.
