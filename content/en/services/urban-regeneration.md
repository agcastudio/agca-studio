A large part of Istanbul's housing stock is being renewed. Urban regeneration is more than replacing a building with a new one: it requires reading the plot's development rights correctly, turning the expectations of existing owners into a plan, and designing usable apartments that relate to the street. Our studio runs this process on the architectural side from start to finish.

## What we do

- **Site and zoning analysis:** survey of the building and plot, zoning status, setbacks, density and height rights.
- **Massing and density studies:** alternatives that fit the plot, number and size of apartments, parking and shared spaces.
- **Schematic design:** plans, sections and visualisations to share with owners; maturing the decisions.
- **Permit-ready construction documents:** an architectural set prepared in Revit and coordinated with structural, mechanical and electrical design.
- **Visualisation and virtual tours:** letting owners and contractors experience apartments and shared spaces before they are built.

## How we work

1. First meeting and collection of plot information.
2. Analysis and alternatives: two or three massing options with a comparison table.
3. Schematic design and owner presentation.
4. Construction documents and discipline coordination.
5. Technical support during the permit process.

We work across Istanbul, with Üsküdar at the centre. Because the project lives in a single Revit model, quantities and revisions stay consistent throughout.
