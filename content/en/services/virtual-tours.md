Reading a space from a plan takes experience; a virtual tour makes that experience available to everyone. A client walks through the apartment before it is built, owners see the shared spaces, a sales office shares a link to explain the project. Design decisions are tested while they are still cheap, before construction begins.

## What we offer

- **360° panoramas:** a full view from selected points.
- **Walkable virtual tours:** panoramas linked together and navigated from the plan.
- **Information points:** labels for materials, dimensions and explanations.
- **Sharing:** a link that opens in the browser; can be embedded in your website or used with a VR headset.

## Where it is used

Owner presentations in urban regeneration, sales and marketing for housing and office projects, jury and client presentations for competitions and concepts, early testing of material and furniture decisions in interior projects.

Tours are produced from the [Revit model](/en/services/revit-architectural-design/) we use for design in our studio in Üsküdar, Istanbul; when the design changes, the tour is updated too. They can be delivered together with [architectural visualisation](/en/services/architectural-visualisation/).
