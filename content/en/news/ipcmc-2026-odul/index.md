The paper "The Evolution of Social Acceptance in Megaprojects: An LLM-Driven Stance Analysis of the Istanbul Airport", written by Kutbeddin Ağca with Prof. Dr. Almula Köksal, received the Best Paper Award at the 9th International Project and Construction Management Conference (IPCMC 2026).

The conference was held in Gaziantep on 16-18 October 2026, hosted by Hasan Kalyoncu University.

The paper examines how social acceptance of a megaproject shifts over its life, taking Istanbul Airport as its case: 141,336 Turkish-language posts are classified with the help of a large language model across the announcement, tender, construction and operation phases.

We [announced the paper](/en/news/ipcmc-2026/) ahead of the conference.
