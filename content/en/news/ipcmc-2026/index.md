Kutbeddin Ağca will present a paper at the 9th International Project and Construction Management Conference (IPCMC 2026). The conference meets in Gaziantep on 16-18 October 2026, hosted by Hasan Kalyoncu University and organised jointly with Yıldız Technical University and Middle East Technical University. This year's theme is rethinking the built environment through artificial intelligence.

The paper, written with Almula Köksal, is titled "The Evolution of Social Acceptance in Megaprojects: An LLM-Driven Stance Analysis of the Istanbul Airport". It examines how social acceptance of a megaproject shifts across the project life cycle, taking Istanbul Airport as its case.

The method runs 141,336 Turkish-language posts from 2010 to 2026 through target-oriented stance analysis assisted by a large language model. The classification is validated against a gold standard prepared by two human coders, and the period is divided into four phases: announcement, tender, construction and operation.

The finding is that acceptance does not mature along a straight line. The balance is at its most negative during the announcement phase, turns to support at tender, slips back into negative territory during construction, and recovers during operation after a troubled first year. The paper proposes an early-warning mechanism that reads social media discourse rather than relying on surveys that arrive too late.
