Competition panels are made to be read at a large scale: pinned to a wall and seen from a distance. We wanted the account of OYUK to exist at the scale of the hand as well, so the project was compiled as a printed magazine.

The magazine follows the order of the panels. First the concept: the building is a solid block of stone, and public space is carved out of it. Then the five states of the hollow, and the cultural route that runs through the city's bazaar, han courtyard and mosque courtyard. Then the design decisions: fill, carve, split, cover.

The plans come next, with the festival courtyard, the flexible hall, the workshops, the library and the administration floors, each with its own use scenarios. The last section covers the facades and the detail of the timber shutters that open and close with the sun.

For the project data, the panels and all the images, see [Oyuk](/en/projects/oyuk/), and for the competition entry, see [OYUK competition entry submitted](/en/news/oyuk-yarisma/).
