A showroom and office on the ground floor of an existing office building for KC Yangın, a company working in fire and security systems. The company's yellow and black identity runs through the whole space: a yellow sign on black cladding on the facade, yellow accent walls and a black slatted ceiling inside, with white floors and light surfaces between them as a quiet background.

The plan takes the visitor from the shopfront straight into the display. Curved white counters carry the products and screens; the long meeting table in the middle is both a presentation and a working surface. Behind, separated by dotted glass partitions, are the closed office, the open office, a coffee corner and the WC. Track spots and round fittings on the ceiling light the display and the table separately.

The design was developed in a Revit model; plan, sections, elevation, axonometrics and renderings were produced from the same model.
