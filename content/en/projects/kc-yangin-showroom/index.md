A showroom and office on the ground floor of an existing building for KC Yangın, a company working in fire and security systems. The company's yellow and black identity runs through the whole space: yellow letters on black cladding on the facade, yellow accent walls and a black slatted ceiling inside, with white walls and a light grey carpet between them as a quiet background.

The plan takes the visitor through the secure entrance straight into the display. Three cylindrical white counters and the display boxes along the north wall carry the products; the screens in the yellow niches present public address, CCTV and intruder alarm systems. The long meeting table in the middle is both a presentation and a working surface. Behind are the four-person open office, the single-person closed office in its dotted glass box, the coffee niche, the rack cabinet and the WC.

The design was developed in a Revit model; plan, sections, elevation, axonometrics and renderings were produced from the same model.
