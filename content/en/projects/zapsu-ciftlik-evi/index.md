The house is reached from the road by a short entrance path; the deck faces away from the road, towards the trees. The entrance facade reads as a single closed storey, with the extended eaves of the roof carried on two slender columns.

The ground floor is one open living space: a 57 m² living room, a 19 m² dining area and a 23 m² island kitchen, backed by a pantry and a cold store, with an entrance hall and cloakroom at the door. The 71 m² deck wraps two sides of the living room and rises over the slope.

On the lower floor a 27 m² painting studio, a 37 m² common room and a 42 m² cinema room with four loungers are buried in the slope and open to the garden through glazing; a wide-stepped stair links the two floors, and storage and a WC sit at the back.

The sections show the mono-pitch roof dropping on the entrance side and rising on the garden side; on the deck facade the lower floor is flanked by rubble stone walls.
