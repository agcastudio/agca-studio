A residential building on a sloping plot in Kordonboyu, Kartal, read as six storeys from the street; the slope adds a garden level at the lower end and the hipped roof a roof floor. The facade is built from two materials: white rendered frames and, inside them, anthracite panels forming the balcony parapets and shifting from floor to floor. A band of planters along the top edge of the panels turns the balconies into a green line when seen from the street, while wide windows keep the light of the rooms.

The plan is organised around a central stair and lift core, with one apartment on each side. The entrance is at the middle of the long facade, into the ground floor at street level. At the lower end of the slope the garden level opens to the garden; the top-floor apartments connect by internal stairs and a gallery void to a roof floor under the hipped roof, with terraces cut into the roof.

The project was developed in a single Revit model; plans, section, elevations and visualisations were produced from the same model.
