A six-storey residential building on a sloping plot in Kordonboyu, Kartal. The facade is built from two materials: a white rendered volume and anthracite panels wrapping the bays on every floor. A band of planters in front of the panels turns the balconies into a green line when seen from the street, while wide windows keep the light and the view of the rooms.

The plan is organised around a central stair core, with rooms on both sides. The ground floor opens to the garden and the roof floor steps back into terraces. Sections and elevations take the sloping ground as it is: the building sits on the land, and the floor lines do not follow the slope of the street.

The project was developed in a single Revit model; plans, sections, elevations and visualisations were produced from the same model.
