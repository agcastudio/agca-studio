A physiotherapy and hydrotherapy centre among pine trees in Bodrum. The building is composed of a low mass of white prisms and slender colonnades running along the facades; courtyards open areas of light and shade between the blocks.

The hydrotherapy pool, under a coffered ceiling, looks onto the garden through a row of columns; the physiotherapy hall is a fully glazed volume with the same ceiling language. The timber-lined sauna opens onto a small gravel courtyard; the rest courtyard has a single tree and shaded seating.

Circulation is kept level and wide with wheelchair users in mind. Plans of the basement, ground and two upper floors, section and elevations were produced from a single model.
