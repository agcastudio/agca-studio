Interior design for a 105 m² three-bedroom apartment in Görele. The limited floor area is answered in each room with a single strong surface: the stone wall that also carries the fireplace in the living room, the veined stone island in the kitchen, the walnut-veneered wardrobe in the hall, patterned wall coverings in the bathrooms. The remaining surfaces are kept light, and a herringbone floor ties all the rooms together.

Lighting is built from concealed strips: along ceiling edges, under cabinets and in mirror niches. The ceiling height is preserved, and in the evening the rooms fill with a soft light. Each bedroom has a desk in front of the window; the master bedroom is resolved together with its wardrobes.

The plan keeps the three-bedroom layout; furniture and joinery were set out to size in a Revit model and visualised from it.
